## Personal data covered

- my name, email address and phone number (if provided);
- information about the recipient of the song and other order data I enter: the recipient's name, our relationship, the occasion, my story, my preferences for the song, and lyrics or edits I submit;
- the lyrics and audio files created for my order;
- payment information received from the payment service provider: payment status, amount, currency, payment identifier and masked card number;

## Purposes

- accepting, paying for and fulfilling my order, including creating, revising and delivering the song;
- sending notifications about my order and, if I subscribe, about recurring payments;

- measuring the effectiveness of advertising and showing relevant ads, if I have accepted analytics and advertising cookies;
- preventing fraud and complying with legal requirements.

## Processing operations

Collection, recording, organization, storage, updating, use, transfer (including cross-border transfer), restriction, deletion and destruction of personal data, with or without automated means.

I agree that my personal data, to the extent necessary for the purposes above, may be transferred to the payment service provider UnitPay; to providers of hosting, cloud storage, email delivery and other technical services; to providers of AI generation services (song order data only); and, if I have accepted analytics and advertising cookies, to Google (Google Analytics, Google Ads), Meta Platforms (Meta Pixel and Conversions API) and Yandex (Yandex Ads, Yandex Metrica). I understand that these recipients may be located outside my country of residence and outside the UAE, including in the United States, the European Union and the Russian Federation, where the level of data protection may differ from my country.

## Data about other people

I confirm that I am entitled to provide the information about the recipient of the song and other people mentioned in my story, and that I provide it only so that the song can be created.

## Marketing communications

If I tick the separate box for marketing communications, I also agree to receive emails with news, offers and promo codes from Mezmo. This consent is optional and is not a condition of my order. I can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

## Retention

I agree that my personal data will be stored for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes above and perform the contract, and longer where required by law, as described in the Privacy Policy.

## Duration and withdrawal

This consent is valid until the purposes of processing are achieved or until I withdraw it. I can withdraw it at any time by writing to [support@mezmo-song.com](mailto:support@mezmo-song.com), and I can withdraw consent to analytics and advertising cookies in the cookie settings on the Site. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before it. After withdrawal, the Operator may continue processing where another legal basis applies, for example to complete a paid order or to meet legal obligations, and fulfillment of an unfinished order may become impossible.
