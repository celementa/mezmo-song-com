# Information Security Policy

Appendix 1 to the Terms of Service. Last updated: October 23, 2026
This Policy describes how Celementa FZCO (registration number 16116518, Dubai Silicon Oasis, DDP, Building A2, 341041, Dubai, UAE) ("we") protects confidential information and cardholder payment details when you pay on the website mezmo-song.com (the "Site").

## Payment processing

Payments on the Site are processed by the payment service provider UnitPay (Unitpay.ru LLC), which is certified to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Level 1, the highest level of certification for organizations that process card payments.
Card details (card number, expiry date, CVV/CVC code and cardholder name) are entered only on the secure payment page of the payment service provider. We do not receive, process or store cardholder card details on our servers, in our databases or in our correspondence.
From the payment service provider we receive only the information needed to confirm the order: payment status, amount, currency, payment identifier and, where available, a masked card number (for example 4111 11** **** 1111).
Card details are never passed to our analytics or advertising partners.

## Secure data transmission

All data between your browser and the Site, and between the Site and the payment service provider, is transmitted over an encrypted connection using TLS (HTTPS). The Site uses a valid SSL/TLS certificate.
Card payments are authenticated with 3-D Secure (Visa Secure, Mastercard Identity Check) where supported by the issuing bank: the payment may require a one-time code from your bank.
Payment status notifications from the payment service provider to our server are transmitted over HTTPS, and we verify each payment against the provider's records before marking an order as paid.

## Recurring payments

When you subscribe, the payment service provider stores a token of your payment method. Card details cannot be recovered from the token. It is used only to charge the subscription price on the terms you agreed to.
After you cancel your subscription, the token is no longer used for charges and is deleted or deactivated within the time set by the payment service provider.

## Storage of confidential information

Customers' personal data (name, email address, phone number and order data) is stored on servers with restricted access. Access is granted only to employees and contractors who need it to fulfill orders and provide support, and is protected by individual credentials and key-based authentication.
Links to order pages contain a signed identifier, so that an order cannot be opened by guessing its number.
Administrative access to our systems is protected by passwords and encryption keys that are not shared with third parties. Repeated failed sign-in attempts are limited.
We make regular backups of data and keep them in secure storage with restricted access.
Personal data is kept no longer than necessary for the purposes set out in the Privacy Policy.

## Confidentiality

We do not disclose confidential information or customer data to third parties, except:

- to the payment service provider, to process payments;
- to providers of hosting, email, AI generation and other technical services, to the extent necessary to fulfill orders;
- to analytics and advertising partners, as described in the Privacy Policy and only for data that does not include card details;
- where required by law or a lawful request from a public authority.

Employees and contractors with access to customer data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## Incidents

If we detect unauthorized access to customer data, we take immediate steps to stop it and limit its consequences. We notify affected customers and the competent authorities where the law requires it, including within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies.

## Recommendations for cardholders

- Pay only on a page whose address starts with "https\://" and has a valid certificate.
- Never share your card's CVV/CVC code or one-time codes from your bank with anyone, including people who claim to be our employees. We never ask for them.
- If you notice a suspicious charge, contact your bank immediately and write to us.
