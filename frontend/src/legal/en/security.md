# Information Security Policy

Appendix 1 to the Terms of Service. Last updated: November 9, 2026
This Policy describes how Celementa FZCO (registration number 16116518, registered by the Dubai Silicon Oasis Authority (DSOA), Dubai Silicon Oasis, DDP, Building A2, 341041, Dubai, UAE) ("we") protects confidential information and cardholder payment details when you pay on the website mezmo-song.com (the "Site").

## 1. Payment processing

Payments on the Site are processed by our payment service providers Stripe or UnitPay (Unitpay.ru LLC), as shown on the payment page. Each provider is certified to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) Level 1, the highest level of certification for organizations that process card payments.
Card details (card number, expiry date, CVV/CVC code and cardholder name) are entered only on the secure payment page of the payment service provider. We do not receive, process or store cardholder card details on our servers, in our databases or in our correspondence.
From the payment service provider we receive only the information needed to confirm the order: payment status, amount, currency, payment identifier and, where available, a masked card number (for example 4111 11** **** 1111).
Card details are never passed to our analytics or advertising partners.

## 2. Secure data transmission

All data between your browser and the Site, and between the Site and the payment service provider, is transmitted over an encrypted connection using TLS (HTTPS). The Site uses a valid SSL/TLS certificate.
Card payments are authenticated with 3-D Secure (Visa Secure, Mastercard Identity Check) where supported by the issuing bank: the payment may require a one-time code from your bank.
Payment status notifications from the payment service provider to our server are transmitted over HTTPS, and we verify each payment against the provider's records before marking an order as paid.

## 3. Recurring payments

When you subscribe, the payment service provider stores a token of your payment method. Card details cannot be recovered from the token. It is used only to charge the subscription price on the terms you agreed to.
After you cancel your subscription, the token is no longer used for charges and is deleted or deactivated within the time set by the payment service provider.

## 4. Storage of confidential information

Customers' personal data (name, email address, phone number and order data) is stored on servers with restricted access. Access is granted only to employees and contractors who need it to fulfill orders and provide support, and is protected by individual credentials and key-based authentication.
Links to order pages contain a signed identifier, so that an order cannot be opened by guessing its number.
Administrative access to our systems is protected by passwords and encryption keys that are not shared with third parties. Repeated failed sign-in attempts are limited.
We make regular backups of data and keep them in secure storage with restricted access.
Personal data is kept no longer than necessary for the purposes set out in the Privacy Policy.

## 5. Confidentiality

We do not disclose confidential information or customer data to third parties, except:

- to the payment service provider, to process payments;
- to providers of hosting, email, AI generation and other technical services, to the extent necessary to fulfill orders;
- to analytics and advertising partners, as described in the Privacy Policy and only for data that does not include card details;
- where required by law or a lawful request from a public authority.

Employees and contractors with access to customer data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## 6. Procedure for protective measures against unauthorised payments

To reduce and control the risk of fraudulent transactions, we use anti-fraud mechanisms and limits, the restrictions and control measures provided for by the internet acquiring agreement, and we interact with the Bank's fraud prevention tools. In this section, "Bank" means the issuing bank, the acquiring bank and/or the settlement bank involved in processing a Payment.

6.1. A Payment is deemed authorised if it is made by the Payer or by a person duly authorised to make the relevant Payment and does not contravene the laws of the United Arab Emirates.

A Payment is also deemed authorised provided that the Payer's instruction has been accepted by the Bank and executed in compliance with the established procedure for protective measures against unauthorised payments.

6.2. A Payment is deemed unauthorised if it is made in breach of the requirements set out in clause 6.1 of this Policy.

Payments made using forged, lost, stolen or compromised payment instruments, or using authentication data obtained unlawfully, are also treated as unauthorised Payments.

6.3. The security procedures for protection against unauthorised Payments, including Authorisation methods and other protective measures, are established by agreements between the parties to the settlements and/or by the rules of the Payment systems through which electronic payment documents are transmitted, and are binding on the Payer.

6.4. The Payer undertakes to take all reasonable and sufficient measures to protect its payment instruments, electronic payment means and authentication data, including logins, passwords, PIN codes, one-time confirmation codes and other identification means, and not to disclose them to third parties.

6.5. All Payments made using the Payer's correct authentication data and that have passed the Authorisation procedures provided for by the Bank, the laws of the United Arab Emirates and/or the Payment systems are deemed authorised, unless proven otherwise in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates.

6.6. The Bank is not liable for unauthorised Payments made as a result of:
- the Payer's breach of the requirements of this Policy, the laws of the United Arab Emirates and the rules of the Payment systems;
- the loss, compromise or disclosure by the Payer to third parties of payment instruments and/or authentication data;
- the impact of malicious software, phishing, social engineering or other actions of third parties outside the Bank's control.

6.7. The Payer undertakes to notify the issuing bank immediately upon discovering the loss of a payment instrument, the compromise of authentication data or a suspicion that an unauthorised Payment has been made. Payments made before the issuing bank blocks the Payer's payment instruments are deemed authorised.

6.8. The Bank may apply additional protective measures, including suspending, restricting or refusing to process Payments, if signs of unauthorised actions are detected, without prior notice to the Payer, where such measures are aimed at preventing possible damage to the Payer and/or the Bank.

## 7. Incidents

If we detect unauthorized access to customer data, we take immediate steps to stop it and limit its consequences. We notify affected customers and the competent authorities where the law requires it, including within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies.

## 8. Recommendations for cardholders

- Pay only on a page whose address starts with "https\://" and has a valid certificate.
- Never share your card's CVV/CVC code or one-time codes from your bank with anyone, including people who claim to be our employees. We never ask for them.
- If you notice a suspicious charge, contact your bank immediately and write to us.
