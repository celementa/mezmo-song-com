# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

## Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Celementa FZCO, a company registered in the Dubai Silicon Oasis free zone, United Arab Emirates, registration number 16116518, address: Dubai Silicon Oasis, DDP, Building A2, 341041, Dubai, UAE ("Mezmo", "we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the website mezmo-song.com (the "Site") and order personalized songs (the "Service").
We are the controller of the personal data described in this Policy. We process personal data in accordance with the laws of the United Arab Emirates, including Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data, and with the mandatory data protection laws of your country of residence where they apply, such as the EU General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the UK GDPR and US state privacy laws.
Questions about this Policy can be sent to [support@mezmo-song.com](mailto:support@mezmo-song.com).

## Summary

- We collect your name, email address, phone number (if provided), information about the song and its recipient, payment information from our payment provider, and technical data about your visit.
- We use this data to create and deliver your song, process payments, support you, keep the Site secure and, where permitted, measure and improve our advertising.
- We use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising tools, Yandex Ads and Google Search Console. Where the law requires it, analytics and advertising cookies are used only with your consent.
- We do not receive or store your full card details.
- We do not sell personal data for money. Some laws treat advertising cookies as "sharing", and you can opt out of them at any time.
- You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent and object to processing by writing to support@mezmo-song.com.

## Personal data we collect

### Data you provide

- **Contact data:** your name, email address and phone number, if you provide them when ordering or contacting us.
- **Song order data:** the recipient's name, your relationship to the recipient, the recipient's gender (to use the correct grammatical forms), the occasion, your story and wishes, the chosen mood, genre or vocal style, any lyrics or edits you submit, and revision requests.
- **Generated content:** the lyrics and audio files created for your order, including earlier versions created through revisions.
- **Payment data:** payments are processed by our payment service provider UnitPay. We do not receive or store full card numbers or security codes. We receive only what is needed to confirm the order: payment status, amount, currency, payment identifier and, where available, a masked card number (for example 4111 11** **** 1111). For subscriptions, the payment provider stores a token of your payment method for recurring charges until you cancel.
- **Subscription data:** subscription status, billing dates, renewal and cancellation history.
- **Communications:** messages you send to customer support and our replies, and feedback you choose to give.

### Data collected automatically

- **Technical and usage data:** IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, language settings, referring page, pages viewed, steps of the order form you reached, date and time of visits, and approximate location derived from your IP address (country or city).
- **Advertising and attribution data:** UTM tags and advertising click identifiers (such as Google's gclid, Meta's fbclid and Yandex's yclid) that show which ad or campaign brought you to the Site, and cookie and similar identifiers set by us and our advertising and analytics partners.
- **Cookies and similar technologies:** see "Cookies and similar technologies" below.

### Data about other people

When you order a song for someone else, you give us information about that person (usually their name, your relationship and details of your shared story). We use it only to create and deliver the song. Please share only what is needed and only if you are entitled to do so. If you are the recipient of a song and want to ask about your data, contact us.

### Sensitive data

We do not ask for sensitive personal data. Please do not include information about health, religion, political views, sexual life or criminal records in your story unless it is essential to the song and the person concerned has agreed. If you include such information, you ask us to process it only to create your song.

## Analytics, advertising and search tools

We use the following third-party tools on the Site. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy, and some act as independent or joint controllers for the data they collect.

- **Google Analytics 4** (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): measures visits, traffic sources and actions on the Site, such as reaching the order form or completing a purchase. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses, and we do not send your name, email or phone number to Google Analytics. Privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can opt out with the [Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- **Google Ads**, including conversion tracking and remarketing: shows our ads in Google Search, YouTube and partner sites, and measures which ads lead to orders. Where enabled, we may send a hashed (encrypted one-way) version of your email address or phone number to improve conversion measurement ("enhanced conversions"). You can manage Google ad personalization at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com).
- **Google Tag Manager**: a tool we use to manage the tags above. It does not itself collect personal data, but it loads the tags that do.
- **Google Search Console**: shows us how the Site appears in Google Search results (search queries, impressions, clicks and indexing). We receive only aggregated statistics that do not identify individual visitors. No cookies are set on the Site by Search Console.
- **Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API** (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited / Meta Platforms, Inc.): measure the effectiveness of our ads on Facebook and Instagram and build audiences of Site visitors. Events such as page views, starting an order and purchases may be sent from your browser and, where enabled, from our server together with a hashed email address or phone number. Meta and Celementa FZCO may be joint controllers for collecting and transmitting this data. Privacy policy: [facebook.com/privacy/policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy). You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.
- **Yandex Ads (Yandex Direct) and Yandex Metrica** (YANDEX LLC and its affiliates): show our ads in Yandex services and partner sites and measure conversions. The Yandex Metrica tag records visits, actions on the Site and conversion events. Privacy policy: [yandex.com/legal/confidential](https://yandex.com/legal/confidential/). You can opt out with the [Yandex Metrica opt-out add-on](https://yandex.com/support/metrica/general/opt-out.html).

Data collected by these tools may be transferred to and stored in the United States, the European Union, the Russian Federation and other countries where the providers operate.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files stored in your browser. We also use similar technologies such as pixels, tags and local storage. We use them in these categories:

- **Strictly necessary:** make the Site work, for example to keep an unfinished order so you do not lose your answers, keep you signed in to your subscription account, remember which version of a page you were shown, apply limits on free song previews, and protect against abuse. These do not require consent.
- **Analytics:** help us understand how the Site is used (Google Analytics, Yandex Metrica).
- **Advertising:** measure ad performance and show you relevant ads on other sites and apps (Google Ads, Meta, Yandex Ads).

Where the law requires it, including for visitors from the EU, EEA and UK, we set analytics and advertising cookies only after you give consent through our cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the cookie settings link at the bottom of the Site, and you can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the Site, such as the order form, from working.

| Cookie | Provider | Category | Purpose | Duration |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Order, session and preference cookies; local storage of the order draft | Mezmo (first party) | Strictly necessary | Keep your unfinished order, sign-in and page version; limit free previews; security | Session to 90 days |
| _ga, _ga_\<ID> | Google Analytics | Analytics | Distinguish visitors and sessions | Up to 2 years |
| _gcl_au, _gcl_aw | Google Ads | Advertising | Conversion measurement for Google Ads | 90 days |
| _fbp, _fbc | Meta | Advertising | Identify browsers and ad clicks for Meta ads measurement | 90 days |
| _ym_uid, _ym_d, _ym_isad and related | Yandex Metrica | Analytics and advertising | Distinguish visitors; measure Yandex Ads conversions | Session to 1 year |

We do not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals because there is no common standard for them. We do honor the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal as an opt-out of advertising cookies where the law requires it.

## How long we keep personal data

- **Order data, songs and lyrics:** while your order page is available to you and for up to 3 years after your last order, so you can return to your song and we can handle support requests and claims.
- **Payment and transaction records:** for the period required by tax and accounting laws, generally up to 5 years.
- **Subscription data:** while the subscription is active and for up to 3 years after it ends. The payment method token is kept by the payment service provider until you cancel.
- **Support correspondence:** up to 3 years after the issue is resolved.
- **Marketing data:** until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent.
- **Analytics and advertising data:** according to the cookie durations listed above and the retention settings of each tool (for Google Analytics, no longer than 14 months for user-level data).

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it. You can ask us to delete your data earlier (see "Your rights").

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- know whether we process your personal data and receive a copy of it;
- correct inaccurate or incomplete data;
- delete your data, unless we must keep it by law;
- restrict processing or object to processing based on legitimate interests, including profiling for direct marketing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. Withdrawing consent to recurring payments is the same as cancelling your subscription;
- opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in each email;
- opt out of targeted advertising and of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data as those terms are defined in some US state laws;
- lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example in your EU member state of residence, with the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or with the UAE Data Office.

To exercise your rights, email [support@mezmo-song.com](mailto:support@mezmo-song.com) from the address used for your order or include your order number. If the law that applies to you sets a different deadline (for example 45 days under some US state laws), we follow it, and we will tell you if we need to extend the deadline and why.
